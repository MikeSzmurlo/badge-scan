Badge to Lead

Point your phone at a badge. When the QR code is steady for a moment the photo snaps on its own and the name, company and badge type are read right on your phone.

  1. Ask first, then point from arm's length or closer. If your iPhone has a zoom lens, tap Far mode to reach a step further.
  2. Aim the center dot at the badge's QR code and hold steady until you see the flash.
  3. Add a note while the conversation is fresh.

Everything stays on this phone. Export your list after each event.
